# ITOM 1.1.0 DEV5 testing RC

DEV5 has directly measured gains over the last DEV4 RC:48/48short sequential phases pass versus42/48 for DEV4, upload average improves in all eight profiles and download in seven. The capped SINGLE processing case improves180.3/91.1→197.0/124.4Mbps. These are matched simulator measurements, not predicted Rambler speed.

Fixes: per-account oldest-first recovery audit pacing; live grants before historical audit traffic; one fair command turn on the shared control socket; fallback bulk classified as DATA instead of urgent; hot response search enabled on a dedicated reader even when writes/cleanup share a socket; startup flight doubles per useful-ACK turn instead of1.5 gain. Quota, journal, wallet, proof, receiver credit, wire and connection-cap authority remain intact. No timeout manufactures a refund or a zero-spend proof for missing history.
