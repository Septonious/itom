# Rev7 transport investigation — 16 October 2026

The supplied client and server captures both identify Rev6. They demonstrate real useful traffic, followed by a correlated physical-socket failure and successful reattachment. They do not establish a provider throughput ceiling or identify why the remote sockets closed. Rev7 addresses the local failure amplification and reproduces additional retained-state capacity problems in the laboratory.

## Control recovery

Rev6 sent a control APPEND error to the carrier supervisor. That tears down the encrypted carrier, receive services, and DATA services even if other sockets remain healthy. Rev7 replaces the failed physical control socket independently. It retains the cipher/session, logical stream owners and journal state. A lost APPEND completion is uncertain: its original counters and physical charge remain discoverable for real receipt and cleanup proofs. A replacement uses fresh authenticated counters and a separately charged journal attempt. Neither repeating old MIME nor clearing debt is a safe substitute.

A regression closes thirty client/server control sockets after an 8 MiB verified transfer on fifteen accounts with retained quota. Unchanged Rev6 exits all thirty carrier actors. The fixed path preserves those actors and the original chat connection, then completes fanout 1/12/1 follow-up transfers. A separate TLS provider test stores a control literal and closes before returning its completion; both physical attempts remain charged and use distinct counters.

The low-session regression additionally found that a background optional DATA repair could take a failed base connection's slot. Control replacement then failed with IMAP_LOCAL_CONNECTION_LIMIT. Physical admission now reserves required per-account and endpoint control capacity. Bounded priority queues at the account and endpoint LOGIN gates allow control/receive repairs ahead of optional retries, while still serving optional work. A fifteen-account test closes all client roles with slow optional logins already queued and requires existing carriers and the original messaging socket to survive. Mixed, socket-capped layouts are tested separately. Scope, credential, certificate and accounting failures are not silently treated as successful repair.

## Handshake reply recovery

If an accepted server hello reply never becomes visible, the server may already have a live carrier while the client waits for the old handshake round. Rev7 retransmits the exact client Noise offer on a bounded schedule. Only the exact previously authenticated offer digest can retrieve the cached server reply; the cipher and epoch remain unchanged. The cache expires after thirty seconds and permits at most three retransmissions, no faster than once per second. Altered, expired and excess replays are tested. Fresh server nonces still reject historical hellos.

## Jitter

239 fully paired client PING/server PONG samples have median 351.91 ms, 95th percentile 672.12 ms and maximum 826.35 ms. Client queue time has median 1.44 ms and 95th percentile 43.51 ms; server reply queue time has median 2.20 ms and 95th percentile 79.21 ms. The largest variation is between publication and the opposite reader consuming mail. These one-way intervals include clock offset and must not be interpreted as a calibrated network propagation measurement; complete RTTs use the same client clock.

Rev7 retains event-driven IDLE and quiet-account pacing. When two actual audits find rows without a preceding IDLE notification, active traffic temporarily uses a 150 ms audit bound instead of 500 ms. Empty polls, local wakes and successful notifications do not manufacture this evidence. The fallback expires after thirty seconds and does not apply to quiet accounts. It changes scheduling only: NOOP/IDLE, polling and timeouts still cannot prove peer progress, mailbox deletion or quota release. A real TLS fixture advertises IDLE while withholding its notifications and verifies the established application's latency; separate tests retain the empty-mailbox command-pressure bound.

Health traces now populate the actual carrier stream count. Earlier zero TCPStreams values in these health records were an observability defect, not evidence that no application streams existed.

## Retained-state throughput and multiplexing

Fresh independent performance runs missed an important workload: an installed server may retain several MiB and most ordinals in old grants while a client has no matching wallet history. Existing lifecycle tests exercised correctness with that state but did not require sustained target throughput.

The added fifteen-account, sixty-second benchmark retains 6 MiB and 130 message slots per account, with real journals/fsync and sparse physical UID history. Before the lease repair, SINGLE averages 35.140 Mbit/s useful upload and 137.857 Mbit/s download; its lowest full/tail measurement is 33.502 Mbit/s. MULTI averages 74.628/103.713 Mbit/s; its lowest measurement is 70.927 Mbit/s. Both fail the unchanged 90 Mbit/s acceptance floor despite all thirty duplex RTT probes succeeding. These are laboratory results, not estimates of the user's link.

A fixed 512 KiB recovery bulk lease can hold only one normal physical batch. Every following batch then waits for grant fencing, publication and cleanup turns, despite genuinely unused byte capacity. Meanwhile the small latency lease could occupy most remaining ordinals. Rev7 divides the remaining ordinals between traffic classes and leases multiple complete bulk copies where the actual charged ledger permits. It retains old charges, the unchanged physical byte/message caps, and local fast/bulk cushions. Near-full 176–188-slot profiles remain covered; they must still bootstrap even when only one bulk lease fits. A red/green journal-and-wallet regression proves consecutive full-size copies are spendable without creating a fresh grant per copy or altering historical debt.

Both fresh and retained-state matrices are required for final qualification: three SINGLE and three 24-stream-per-direction MULTI runs per matrix, with fifteen accounts, ten-second warmup and sixty-second measurement. Every run independently needs both useful directions at least 90 Mbit/s across its full interval, final thirty seconds and final ten seconds, all loaded RTTs below 1,100 ms, all thirty probes overlapping duplex load, and no misses or timeouts. Final results and source identity are recorded in the qualification evidence, not inferred from an exploratory pass.

The lease repair alone improves exploratory SINGLE to 66.467/112.967 Mbit/s and MULTI to 101.673/96.897 Mbit/s; SINGLE still fails. A separate profiled run identifies repeated full origin-balance scans in journal admission at about twenty percent of CPU. The bounded read view reuses a decoded grant only when the exact persisted row is unchanged and ties aggregate totals to the bbolt transaction ID. Durable admission, physical proof and refund continue to use the database; poisoned/lost-commit outcomes cannot return cached success. Tests compare the view with actual rows through uncertainty, fencing, proof cleanup, rollback and reopening.

The read optimization alone still does not qualify SINGLE. A further retained-state admission regression shows that the static outbound share can take capacity freed by an incoming lease before its replacement arrives. The recovery shares now retain all historical charges, then partition only the remaining byte capacity between directions in complete two-bulk-lease units, assigning the incomplete pair to local writes. Remaining message slots are shared evenly, with the bounded minimum local latency/bulk slots retained. Local and remote message headroom also remain bounded. A journal test fences unused incoming leases, fills outgoing capacity, and verifies that the incoming replacement leases still fit without borrowing their share or changing old debt. Fresh profiles retain the established shares. Final speed results, rather than these intermediate gains, determine qualification.

Recovery lease byte limits use whole 512 KiB physical-BODY units, avoiding nearly a whole unusable copy of residual permission per lease. A 512 KiB / 32-slot reduced bulk lease would alias the protocol's historical implicit fast-grant marker; the class-safe reduced lease uses 31 slots instead. A dedicated regression reproduces the resulting grant-queue failure and verifies the fix. No protocol marker or wallet authority is reinterpreted.

An exploratory sixteen-worker scheduler did not improve sustained multiplexing and was reverted. The original eight-worker bound rotates over the account pool; it does not exclude accounts from service. Both handoff queues share the original total budget. In a smaller retained outbound window, local cleanup starts before the window fills, using the existing exact physical proofs.

A full journal revealed a separate wait-loop defect: a failed quota probe released only a shutdown guard but broadcast a capacity notification. Its own waiter immediately probed again (1,149 false wakes in 100 ms in the red test). Failed probes no longer broadcast nonexistent returned capacity. Real capacity changes wake immediately, and cold peer scheduling probes retain a bounded advisory recheck. The regression requires zero false wakes and prompt progress after an actual authenticated unused-grant fence.

Intermediate benchmarks remain failures. The larger leases, read view and directional partition each resolved a reproduced defect but did not independently satisfy every sustained direction/tail. They are not release evidence. A profiled MULTI run then showed small physical batches and a queue-age regression: an old first record expired bulk coalescing before the following socket read became schedulable. Bulk collection with an independent fast worker now gets one bounded collection turn; fast collection retains its absolute queue-age deadline. A real collector regression changes from one record to all eleven records in the next burst. Final qualification must cover the combined candidate.

The MULTI snapshots also expose ordinal pressure: many accounts reach fourteen local message reservations while holding only about 1–2 MiB of the then-available 3 MiB local byte share. Small copies can therefore exhaust ordinals while byte headroom remains idle. A separate forced worker-overtake regression demonstrates a fragmentation mechanism: the delivery controller requires in-order stream prefixes, but parallel workers could reach admission out of collection order. A later mixed-stream batch then selected only its currently eligible members and parked the others, generating many small BODYs and increasing command/ordinal pressure. The collector now performs the nonblocking logical flight decision in collection order and returns a physical continuation. Durable grant admission, encryption and network IO remain outside the collector lock and parallel. The independent fast worker is unchanged. A forced worker-overtake regression fails with the second batch admitted first and verifies both ordered admission and parallel physical writes after the change. Cancellation before physical work must release the unspent logical reservation; no physical charge is refunded by this scheduling change.

Tracing the complete admission decision distinguishes this from slow collection: successful collectors supplied eleven records, but most physical continuations selected only one to five. In the sampled steady-state trace, all omitted records failed the stream-offset predecessor check; none failed the window, per-stream share, or repair constraints. An exploratory longer bulk collection window did not resolve this and was reverted. The original twenty-millisecond bound remains.

Two additional scheduling defects sustained the offset gaps. First, ready retries of previously unstarted records reentered behind speculative future offsets already queued. The scheduler now gives those ready retries priority and holds younger records of a stream while an older record waits for its admission predicate. Sibling streams remain runnable. Both bulk queues share the original 128-record/four-MiB handoff budget, and the logical session bound is unchanged. Second, omitted records waited for the selected sibling's slow physical APPEND before returning to the owner. Ordered admission now returns definitive pre-publication completions immediately, separately from the selected physical continuation. A rejected predecessor can reenter while the accepted sibling is still writing. Cancellation still executes the selected continuation's cleanup once; unknown physical outcomes remain charged. Forced regressions fail on the original ordering, sibling fairness and delayed-completion paths, then pass under the race detector with the changes. All final lifecycle and repeated sustained gates remain required.

## Release boundary

All internal tests, race checks, field repetitions, Android tests/lint/native builds, IMAP fork checks, and both speed matrices must pass on the same source before assets can be packaged. Earlier failed runs remain failed and are preserved separately. GitHub Actions assembles and verifies those exact APK and Linux binaries, then checks uploaded SHA-256 digests before publication. This investigation does not claim to prove the cause of the earlier mobile process crash or guarantee an external Speedtest server's result.

## Final qualification policy

The user explicitly waived the 90 Mbps floor on 2026-10-16 and prioritized stability and usable application connections after load. Rev7 records measured rates without treating them as a performance certification. All other internal correctness, recovery, accounting, race, Android and RTT gates remain mandatory. Every one-second load tick needs useful duplex progress, and the original chat socket plus a fresh verified 256 KiB exchange must work after the load stops without restarting either endpoint. The final evidence records four fresh/retained load runs on one source fingerprint.

## Minimum DATA service recovery found during finalization

An additional repetition exposed a five-second application recovery timeout after simultaneous client socket failures. Control repair alone was insufficient: the sole DATA receiver and the first usable DATA writer could join optional LOGIN queues, while the scheduler correctly refused lanes with no DATA writer. Their restoration now uses critical repair admission; extra parallel writers remain ordinary and paced. The targeted failure scenario passed three consecutive runs after both minimum services were prioritized. The final complete core/Android build and reduced fresh/retained load checks use this corrected runtime. At the user’s request, redundant standalone field repetitions and the added commit-delay experiment were removed; they are not claimed as final passes.
